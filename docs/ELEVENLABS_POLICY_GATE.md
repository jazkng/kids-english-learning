## Required evidence before enabling

1. Provider's written approval identifying this 2–5-year-old educational app, prerecorded offline distribution, Android/PWA and intended markets, and permitted voice approach.
2. Voice rights and any performer consent; no real minor uploads or likeness. Explicit clarification of the requested cartoon child-like aesthetic, or owner-approved change to a permitted alternative.
3. Commercial and redistribution license for generated files and the intended plan/model/voice; attribution obligations and limits verified. Free output must not silently enter a commercial release.
4. Owner's explicit spending decision before any paid enrollment, credit use that incurs charges, automatic top-up or billing-enabled trial. Approval of product policy is not approval of cost.
5. Adult content/listening review and intended-region privacy/store assessment. Actual provider/legal approval evidence is kept privately; public metadata contains only non-sensitive evidence references.
